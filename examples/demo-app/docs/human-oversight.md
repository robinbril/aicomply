# Human oversight

- The assistant only produces a score and a short rationale. It never sends a rejection.
- A recruiter reviews every candidate scored below 40 and records the decision with their name.
- Recruiters can override any score; overrides are logged in the ATS with a reason.
- A kill switch (`SCREENING_ENABLED=false`) disables the assistant; the Head of Talent and the CISO can flip it.
