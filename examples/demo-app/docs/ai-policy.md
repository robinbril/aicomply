# AI policy

Approved by the management board on 2026-09-15 (minutes MB-2026-09, item 4). Reviewed yearly.

1. AI is used at Acme to support, never replace, recruitment decisions. Every rejection that an AI
   score contributed to is confirmed by a named recruiter.
2. AI systems are registered in the AI inventory (`docs/ai-inventory.md`) before use.
3. Personal data is only sent to an AI provider under a signed processor agreement.
4. The CISO owns AI security; the DPO owns AI privacy; the Head of Talent owns the screening use case.
5. Staff who use the screening assistant complete the AI literacy module before access is granted.
